FAQ

Questions

Still unsure? Email us and we'll help.

Do you take my car, keys or title?
Never. You stay the seller of record the whole time. Your car stays in your driveway — we only handle the listing, the buyers and the negotiation. You meet the buyer once, for the handoff, at a price you approved.
What do you do with my VIN?
Price research and listing details only — year, trim, options, market value. Your VIN is never posted publicly in the ad.
When do you start?
Once we have your photos, car details, and payment. If photos are too dark or blurry to fix, we'll ask for reshoots.
How do I pay?
Zelle on the booking page. Basic is paid upfront; Premium can be split $30 now, $30 on delivery. Card payments are coming soon.
What if I'm not happy?
Listing revisions are included (1 round Basic, 2 rounds Premium). Cancel before work starts for a full refund, 50% after work starts. No refunds based on whether the car sells.
Do you guarantee my car will sell?
No one can promise that — and anyone who does is lying. What we promise: your listing looks its best, your buyers get handled, and no offer is ever accepted unless you approved the price.
How does the title transfer work?
You sign the title over to the buyer at the handoff — only after payment is verified. Keep a photo of the signed title and a bill of sale with the buyer's name, the price, and the date. In Texas, the plates stay with you, not the car, and the buyer has 30 days to transfer the title. If anything about your situation is unusual (out-of-state title, name mismatch, lost title), tell us before we list. General information, not legal advice — your local DMV can confirm the details.
How do I get paid safely?
Our fee is separate ($40 Basic / $60 Premium, paid by Zelle on the booking page). For the car itself, meet at a bank: cash or a cashier's check, funds verified before you sign the title. Never accept an overpayment check, a mailed check from a "shipper," or a payment plan from a stranger. We screen buyers and schedule the handoff, but the money changes hands between you and the buyer, in person.
How do test drives work?
You ride along — always. A buyer shows their license, you agree on a short daytime route in advance, and you go with them. We schedule the drive and set expectations beforehand, but the drive itself is yours to supervise. If a buyer won't show a license or wants the car alone, that's a no — we'll tell them so.
What happens if a buyer flakes or ghosts?
It happens — it's the most normal part of selling a car, and it's exactly why sellers hire us. We follow up, keep the listing live, and move on to the next buyer without you ever seeing the ghosting. Premium includes a day-7 refresh; an extra refresh is $10 on either package. The fee covers the work, not a guaranteed sale.
What cars qualify?
Almost any car you own and can photograph: daily drivers, high-mileage cars, project cars — as long as the condition is disclosed honestly. Two things you must tell us upfront: whether there's a lien or loan on the car, and whether the title is salvage, rebuilt, or branded. The one hard rule: it has to be your car to sell.
Do you post from my account or yours?
We post the listing for you — on Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, and OfferUp — and buyer messages come to us first, not to your phone. Nothing is posted without the details you sent us, and nothing about the price changes without your approval. You'll never be asked to hand over a password or log us into your accounts.
How long until my car is listed — and until it sells?
We start as soon as we have your payment, photos, and details. Premium starts within 24 hours. There's no fixed sell-by date: price, condition, and the local market decide that. What keeps a listing from going stale is the day-7 refresh (Premium included, $10 add-on) and honest photos and pricing up front.
What if my car still has a loan on it?
Tell us before we list — it changes how the handoff works, not whether you can sell. Usually the buyer and seller meet at the lienholder's bank: the buyer pays, the loan is paid off, and the title is released for transfer. We'll coordinate the messaging and timing; what we need from you upfront is the payoff amount (or a good estimate) and the lender's name.
Can I sell from outside Houston?
Yes. The whole service is remote — you send photos and details, we run the listing, the buyers, and the negotiation online. The only in-person moment is the handoff, wherever the car is. We're based in Spring, TX, and serve sellers anywhere in the U.S.
