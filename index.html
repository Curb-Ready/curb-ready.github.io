FAQ

Questions

Still unsure? Email us and we'll help.

When do you start?
Once we have your photos, car details, and payment. If photos are too dark or blurry to fix, we'll ask for reshoots.
How do I pay?
Zelle on the booking page. Premium can be split $30 now, $30 later; Local Full-Service is paid upfront. Card payments are coming soon.
What if I'm not happy?
Listing revisions are included (1 round Basic, 2 rounds Premium). Cancel before work starts for a full refund, 50% after work starts. No refunds based on whether the car sells.
Do you guarantee my car will sell?
No one can promise that — and anyone who does is lying. What we promise: your listing looks its best, your buyers get handled, and no offer is ever accepted unless you approved the price.
