FAQ

Questions

Still unsure? Email us and we'll help.

When does the clock start?
Once we have your photos, car details, and payment. If photos are too dark or blurry to fix, we'll ask for reshoots and the clock restarts.
How do I pay?
Zelle or card via Stripe. Basic is paid upfront; Premium can be split $30 now, $30 on delivery.
What if I'm not happy?
Revisions are included (1 round Basic, 2 rounds Premium). Cancel before work starts for a full refund, 50% after work starts. No refunds based on whether the car sells.
Do you guarantee my car will sell?
No — we make your listing the best version of itself. We don't sell the car or promise a price or timeline, and we take no commission.
