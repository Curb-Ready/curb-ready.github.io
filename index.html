What to send us

The shot list

Wash and vacuum the car, clear out personal items, and shoot in open shade or near sunrise/sunset. Hold your phone horizontally at headlight height, flash off, no pinch-zoom. Walk closer instead.

Exterior (8)

  1. Front 3/4, driver side (usually the cover)
  2. Front, straight on
  3. Driver side profile
  4. Rear 3/4, passenger side
  5. Rear, straight on
  6. Passenger side profile
  7. Front 3/4, passenger side
  8. Wheel & tire close-up (show tread)

Interior (7)

  1. Dashboard from the back seat, centered
  2. Driver seat & door, door open
  3. Instrument cluster with the car running
  4. Center console, screen & shifter
  5. Rear seats
  6. Front passenger seat
  7. Headliner or sunroof

Details (5+)

  1. Trunk or cargo area (open)
  2. Engine bay
  3. Keys or fobs
  4. Every dent, scratch, rust spot or tear, close up
  5. Anything special: new tires, upgrades, receipts
Send everything. We'll pick the best shots for your tier and put them in the strongest order. Use Google Drive, Dropbox or a shared album.

FAQ

Questions

Still unsure? Email us and we'll help.

When does the 24–48 hour clock start?
Once we have all three: your photos, your car details, and payment (or the deposit for a Premium 50/50 order). If photos are too blurry or dark to fix, we'll ask for reshoots and the clock restarts.
How do I pay?
Zelle or card via a Stripe payment link (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay). Basic is paid upfront. Premium can be paid in full, or $30 now and $30 before final files are delivered.
What's your refund policy?
  • Full refund if you cancel before work starts.
  • 50% refund if you cancel after work starts but before delivery.
  • After delivery, we'll make it right with your included revisions. If it still isn't usable, we'll issue a partial or full refund at our discretion.
  • No refunds based on whether, or for how much, the car sells.
Do you guarantee my car will sell?
No. Curb Ready improves your photos and listing. We don't sell the car or guarantee a price or timeline, and we don't take a commission.
What counts as a revision?
Changes to the delivered work, like copy tweaks or photo re-edits. Basic includes 1 round, Premium includes 2. A revision isn't a new set of photos.
Will you hide dents or damage?
Never. Honest photos build trust with buyers and save everyone wasted trips. We fix light, color and framing, not the car.
Who owns the photos?
You do, originals and edits. We only use them as portfolio examples if you say yes, and only with plates blurred.
What happens to my info and files?
Your info is used only for your order. Files are deleted 60 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them. Share your city or neighborhood only, never a home address.
