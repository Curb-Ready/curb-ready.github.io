FAQ

Houston sellers ask

The short answers. The full list lives on the homepage.

Do you take my car, keys or title?
Never. You stay the seller of record the whole time. Your car stays in your driveway — we only handle the listing, the buyers and the negotiation. You meet the buyer once, for the handoff, at a price you approved.
How do I get paid safely?
Meet at a bank: cash or a cashier's check, with funds verified before you sign the title. Never accept an overpayment check, a mailed check from a "shipper," or a payment plan from a stranger. Our fee is separate ($40 / $60, paid by Zelle on the booking page) — the sale price itself changes hands between you and the buyer, in person.
How does the title transfer work in Texas?
You sign the title over at the handoff, only after payment is verified. Keep a photo of the signed title and a bill of sale with the buyer's name, price, and date. Texas plates stay with you, not the car. The buyer has 30 days to transfer the title into their name. General information, not legal advice — your local DMV can confirm the details.
Do you guarantee my car will sell?
No one can promise that — and anyone who does is lying. What we promise: your listing looks its best, your buyers get handled, and no offer is ever accepted unless you approved the price.
